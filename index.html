Technology Comedy Club, IIT Kharagpur, Welcomes you!

Hey there, tech enthusiasts, code wizards, and laughter seekers! We are thrilled to welcome you to the ultimate destination for all things comedy - the Technology Comedy Club at IIT Kharagpur! Imagine a bunch of tech-enthusiasts IIT KGP whiz-kids combining their love for technology with a side of humor. Well, you don't have to imagine anymore, because you've just stumbled upon the IIT Laughs Hub, where coding meets comedy, bytes become giggles, and debugging is as entertaining as it gets! Our journey began as a bunch of friends with a shared passion for both bits and jokes. Now, we've created a space where the world of technology and laughter collide in the most unexpected and hilarious way. Whether you're a seasoned maggu, a casual tech enthusiast, or just someone looking for a good laugh, you're in the right place. Expect mind-blowing stand-up, witty programming jokes, and perhaps a few unexpected surprises along the way. Get ready to live, laugh, and repeat! Thanks for dropping by, let the Laughs begin! — Technology Comedy Club, IIT Kharagpur
